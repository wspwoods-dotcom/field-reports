About this prototype

Field Reports is a working prototype for Greene County Conservation's maintenance reporting.

  • 📴 Everything stays on this phone. There is no backend, no account, and no syncing in this prototype — that arrives in Phase 2.
  • 📶 It works fully offline, including in dead zones. Reports save locally the moment you hit send.
  • 🧾 Closed jobs log labor, equipment, and materials so the Savings view can show industry value vs. in-house cost.
  • 🗺️ The park list comes from mycountyparks.com and is editable above.
